<h2>Final Reports and Experimental Histories</h2>

<p>
This folder contains the final explainability reports and experimental histories generated during each LLM-guided CMA-ES run.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>
    <b>Final reports:</b> explanations produced by the Final Explainer that summarize the selected CMA-ES variant and reconstruct its design trajectory.
  </li>
  <li>
    <b>Experimental histories:</b> accumulated records of the evaluated variants, BBOB results, comparison labels, best-so-far selections, and feedback generated throughout the optimization cycles.
  </li>
</ul>

<p>
Together, these files provide traceability of the complete evolution of each run and the experimental evidence supporting the final selected variant.
</p>
